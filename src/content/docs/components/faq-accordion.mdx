Our documentation includes a custom, reusable `<FAQ>` component that allows you to create interactive, collapsible accordion menus[cite: 5]. This is perfect for organizing long troubleshooting pages or grouping common billing questions[cite: 5].

---

## Behind the Curtain: FAQ Component

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="1. Rendered Specimen" icon="seti:html">
    *How the interactive accordion renders on the live site.*

    <FAQ title="What is your refund policy?">
    We offer a full refund within the first **30 days** of your subscription. 

    To request a refund, please contact Support.
    </FAQ>
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="2. Underlying MDX Code" icon="seti:markdown">
    *The raw component syntax required to generate the accordion.*

    ```mdx
    import FAQ from '../../../components/FAQ.astro';

    <FAQ title="What is your refund policy?">
    We offer a full refund within the first **30 days** of your subscription. 

    To request a refund, please contact Support.
    </FAQ>
    ```
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="3. Sveltia CMS Authoring" icon="setting">
    *How authors insert FAQs within the CMS.*

    To use the FAQ component in your article, you must first import it at the top of your document, and then wrap your content in the `<FAQ>` tags[cite: 5]. Provide the question inside the `title=""` attribute, and place the answer between the opening and closing tags[cite: 5]. You can use standard Markdown (like bolding, lists, and links) inside the answer[cite: 5]!
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="caution">
Because this is a custom component, add this exact line to the very top of your article: `import FAQ from '../../../components/FAQ.astro';`[cite: 5]. Ensure the relative path matches the folder depth of your current document!
</Aside>
