## How to Use Icons in Your Content

<Steps>
1. **Import the Component:** Include `import { Icon } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';` at the top of your `.mdx` file.
2. **Render Inline:** Call the component anywhere in your body copy: `<Icon name="rocket" />`.
3. **Pass Attributes:** Customize size, color, or accessibility labels like `<Icon name="star" color="accent" label="Favorite" />`.
</Steps>
